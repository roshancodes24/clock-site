Questions about pricing

What is included in Premium?

World Clock, Alarms, and Timer, plus future updates. The stopwatch is free for everyone.

What does Lifetime mean?

Lifetime is $45 paid once. There are no recurring payments. It includes the same premium features and future updates.

Do I get future updates?

Yes. Future updates are included with both the annual plan and Lifetime.

Can I cancel the annual plan?

You can cancel renewal so the next year is not billed. The current year remains $12. Lifetime is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.
