Privacy policy for the Clock browser extension

Clock keeps your clocks, alarms, stopwatch, and timer on this device. Premium is purchased on a secure checkout page. Clock does not store your card number, and it does not sell personal information. This policy was last updated on 25 September 2026.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Clock browser extension and these public website pages. The extension source code is kept in a private repository.

Information stored on your device

Clock uses the browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). That data stays in your browser profile. Clock stores:

  • The time zones you add to World Clock, such as the city and time-zone identifier.
  • Alarms you create: the time, optional name, on or off state, and the next scheduled time.
  • Stopwatch state: whether it is running, elapsed time, and lap times.
  • Timer state: the duration you entered and whether the countdown is running, paused, or finished.
  • Whether premium is active for this browser, so World Clock, Alarms, and Timer can stay unlocked after you pay.

Removing Clock from the browser deletes this extension storage with the extension.

How alarms and the timer use the current page

Clock asks for permission to run on normal http and https pages so it can blur the page you are viewing. When an alarm rings, that overlay shows the alarm name, time, Snooze, and Stop. When a timer finishes, the overlay shows 00:00, Close, and “Time's up!”, then removes itself after 4 seconds.

The overlay script does not read the text of the page, your passwords, form entries, or browsing history. It only draws the Clock card and listens for Snooze, Stop, or Close.

Permissions the extension requests

  • Storage saves the items listed above on your device.
  • Alarms wakes the extension when an alarm or timer is due, including after the popup is closed.
  • Scripting and site access place the alarm and timer card on open web pages.
  • Notifications is declared in the extension manifest. The current Clock version does not send system notifications for alarms or the timer. Those alerts appear on the web page instead.

Premium payment

You can buy premium from inside Clock. Checkout opens on a secure payment page. You enter payment details there, not in the extension. Clock does not collect or store your card number, and it does not keep a copy of your full payment credentials.

The payment provider processes the transaction and may receive details such as your email address and payment method under its own privacy policy. Clock receives a confirmation of whether premium is active so it can unlock World Clock, Alarms, and Timer. The stopwatch does not require payment.

Information Clock does not collect

Clock does not ask you to create a Clock account. It does not include advertising or analytics trackers. It does not send your time zones, alarms, stopwatch laps, or timer settings to be sold. Those items stay in extension storage on your device.

How long information is kept

Extension data remains until you delete an alarm or time zone, reset the stopwatch or timer, or uninstall Clock. Clock does not keep a separate copy.

Children

Clock is a general time utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

How to contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to the person who maintains the Clock extension.
